Privacy policy · Support · Purchase information

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026. App: Ternpace: Focus Launcher (com.orbickk.ternpace). Publisher: Orbickk_Lab.

Your home stays on your device

Ternpace stores your selected app component identifiers, home lists, hidden app lists, short intentions, display preferences, language and weekly routine settings on your device. It reads launchable app names and identifiers for this Android user to display and open them. These app lists and intentions are not sent to us or to our purchase provider.

Ternpace does not read contacts, messages, notifications or other apps’ screens. It does not request accessibility, usage access, location, microphone, camera or broad storage access. Hiding an app only removes it from this launcher’s selected profile; it does not block notifications, links or other launchers.

Optional purchases

Google Play processes payment for Ternpace Plus. RevenueCat validates and restores purchase access. When purchase services are enabled, they process an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and transaction history, store account purchase information and technical information needed for this service, such as app and operating system version and network request information. We do not receive your full payment card details. We do not configure advertising attribution or send app lists or intentions to RevenueCat. No advertising or separate analytics SDK is used.

These providers have their own policies: RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. Processing can take place in countries other than your own. Purchases are optional; the free launcher works without buying Plus.

Backups and removal

You may export a readable JSON backup using Android’s file picker. It contains settings, app identifiers and intentions, so keep the file private. A cloud provider may appear in your system file picker; saving there is your choice and follows that provider’s policy. Ternpace does not automatically upload backups. Backups do not contain a purchase entitlement.

Reset home configuration in the app removes layouts, hidden lists and routines. Android’s Clear storage or uninstall removes app-local settings. Copies you exported remain until you delete them. Store transaction records are separate and may be retained by the payment providers for their legal and accounting obligations.

To request deletion of purchase-related data processed for Ternpace, contact the support address below with the app name and, if you have one, the order identifier. Do not send card details, passwords or service credentials. We may request information needed to identify the record and route the request to the relevant provider. We do not offer user accounts in Ternpace.

Children and changes

Ternpace is a general productivity tool and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request children’s personal details. Contact us if you believe purchase-related data needs attention. We will update this page when data practices change and show the revised effective date.

Support

Email orbickk2048@gmail.com with “Ternpace” in the subject. Include the Android version, phone model and a description of the issue. Avoid including private app lists or intentions unless necessary.

To return to your previous home: open Ternpace → Settings → Switch home app, then choose your old launcher. You can also use Android Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app. Names vary by device. The Phone / Home app shortcut on Ternpace’s home also opens the recovery menu.

Ternpace currently displays launchable apps for the current Android user. Its Personal, Work and Evening profiles are personal layouts, not Android enterprise work accounts. OEM gesture and recent-app behavior may differ between devices.

Purchase information

The free launcher includes home lists, search, manual profiles, hiding, appearance settings, language selection and local backup. Plus is an optional one-time purchase for automatic weekly routines and per-profile names and text sizes. The current local price is shown by Google Play before confirmation. No subscription is offered.

Buying and restoring require an internet connection. A recently verified Plus entitlement can be used offline for up to 72 hours before another online check is needed. Refunds or revocation remove Plus after verification; saved home configurations are kept and manual use remains available. Google Play’s refund rules apply. We do not guarantee support for every future device or Android version, a reduction in screen time or any particular wellbeing outcome.

The app and icon are original Ternpace materials. Third-party open-source components and their licenses are listed in the app under Settings → Licenses & version.
